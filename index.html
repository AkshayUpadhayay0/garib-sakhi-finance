About us

Rooted in Manglour, built for people

Garib Sakhi Finance Pvt. Ltd. is based in Village Baswakheri, Post Manglour, District Haridwar, Uttarakhand. We are a financial services enterprise focused on financial leasing and finance support for small needs that banks often overlook.

The word Sakhi means “friend” — and that is how we aim to work: clear terms, respectful service and no hidden surprises.
